Financial Control & Governance

Key financial administration principles.

◇ Revenue Integrity

Financial income must be traceable to recorded transactions and approved accounting classifications.

⇄ Transaction Accountability

Payment records must retain references, processing history and transaction outcomes.

⚖ Financial Authorization

Refunds and privileged financial operations must be authorized and audited.

◎ Global Currency Oversight

Transactions retain their original currencies and use documented conversion rules where consolidated reporting is required.
