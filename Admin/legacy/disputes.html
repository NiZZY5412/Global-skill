Fairness, Impartiality & Resolution Governance

All parties must be treated fairly and consistently. A dispute submission represents a claim requiring review and is not proof of wrongdoing. Evidence must be assessed in context, relevant responses must be considered, conflicts of interest must be managed, and decisions must be documented with clear reasons. Appeals must follow the applicable review procedure. All sensitive information and administrative interventions must be handled under appropriate privacy, access-control and audit requirements.
