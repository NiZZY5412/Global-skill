Responsible Matching & Administrative Oversight

TALVORA's matching system supports the connection of human capability to employment opportunities. Matching recommendations are decision-support outputs, not automatic employment decisions. Employees must be evaluated against relevant job requirements, and employers retain their authorized recruitment responsibilities. Super Admin interventions must follow established permissions, privacy requirements and documented audit procedures.
