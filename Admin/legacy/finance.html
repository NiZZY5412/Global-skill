Financial Data Integrity

All amounts must come from verified payment, billing and expense records. Pending payments are not counted as collected cash.

Financial totals must be grouped by currency. Taxes, recognized revenue, processor settlements and profitability require separate accounting treatment.

Payment secrets and complete card information must never be stored in this frontend.
