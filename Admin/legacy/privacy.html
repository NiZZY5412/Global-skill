DATA PROTECTION GOVERNANCE

Platform Privacy Controls

Consent and lawful processing

Consent records must preserve the purpose, applicable lawful basis, collection context, version and withdrawal history. Consent should not be treated as the lawful basis for every processing activity.

Data subject rights

Privacy requests must follow the applicable jurisdictional procedures, identity verification requirements, response deadlines and documented resolution workflows.

Consent lifecycle management
