Privacy Governance Principles

All personal information processing must have a documented purpose and an applicable lawful basis. Consent must be specific, informed, freely given where consent is relied upon, and capable of withdrawal. Data subject requests, security incidents, data-sharing arrangements and retention decisions must be documented and auditable.

Administrative access does not automatically authorize unrestricted access to personal information. Apply least-privilege permissions, identity verification, confidentiality safeguards and appropriate review procedures.
